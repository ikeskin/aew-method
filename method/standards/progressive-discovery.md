# Progressive Discovery and Concise Output

- Search existing code, tests and documentation before asking questions.
- Ask only for unresolved decisions that block safe implementation; group at most a few related critical questions at once.
- Defer speculative edge cases until they affect the current scope; do not enumerate every conceivable business rule.
- Keep reports short by default: decision, evidence, change, verification, remaining blockers. Expand only when requested or required by risk.
- Do not hide security, data-loss or compatibility risks for brevity.
